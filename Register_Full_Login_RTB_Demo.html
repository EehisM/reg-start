Verified rule library

Primary source: RTB.ie

1. Annual RTB registration

Register a new tenancy within one month of its start. Re-register annually on the anniversary of the tenancy start date. RTB says annual registration may be completed up to one calendar month after the due date before late fees apply.

RTB: When to register ↗

2. Registration fees

For private, student-specific and cost-rental tenancies, standard registration is €40/year; late fee is €10 per month or part-month late. RTB also describes composite and repeat-registration fee rules.

RTB: Registration fees ↗

3. National rent control from 1 March 2026

All private tenancies and SSA are covered by national rent-control rules. Most annual increases are capped at 2% or CPI, whichever is lower. Qualifying newly built apartments/SSA can increase in line with CPI without the 2% ceiling.

RTB: Setting and reviewing private rents ↗

4. Market-rent resets

Existing private tenancies begun before 1 March 2026 cannot reset to market rent while ongoing. New private tenancies from 1 March 2026 have specific permitted routes, including some new-tenancy circumstances and the end of a six-year Tenancy of Minimum Duration.

RTB rent guidance ↗

5. Rent notices and comparables

RTB guidance requires rent setting/review notices to be sent to the tenant and RTB on the same day. The guidance also requires three comparable properties from the RTB Rent Register in relevant statements.

RTB: Rent review notices ↗

6. Notice of Termination periods

General landlord notice periods are 90 days (<6 months), 152 days (6–12 months), 180 days (1–7 years), 196 days (7–8 years), and 224 days (>8 years). Specific shorter periods apply to certain breaches.

RTB: Notice of Termination guide ↗

7. Ending tenancies from 1 March 2026

For post-1 March 2026 tenancies, permitted termination grounds after six months depend on whether the landlord has 1–3 tenancies or 4+ / is a company, and for small landlords also on the point in the six-year tenancy cycle.

RTB: Ending a tenancy ↗
